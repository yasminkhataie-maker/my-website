## What does a marketing strategist actually do?

A marketing strategist works on the thinking before the doing. In my work, that comes down to five things:

1. **Listen.** Understand the business, its buyers and what leadership wants marketing to achieve.
2. **Diagnose.** Find where the current marketing breaks: the message, the audience, the offer or the system behind it.
3. **Define the narrative.** Agree what the company should be known for, in words a buyer would use.
4. **Design the system.** Decide priorities, channels, budget split and how results will be measured.
5. **Guide execution.** Hand a clear plan to the in-house team or an agency, and check it stays on track.

The output is not a pile of posts or ads. It is clarity: what to do, in what order and why.

## How is a marketing strategist different from an agency or a marketing manager?

They answer different questions. A strategist decides **what** and **why**. An agency and a marketing manager make it happen.

| | Marketing strategist | Marketing agency | Marketing manager |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Main question | What should we say, to whom, and why? | How do we produce and run it? | How do we deliver it every week? |
| Typical output | Diagnosis, narrative, priorities, plan | Ads, content, websites, campaigns | Day-to-day execution and reporting |
| Best time to bring in | Before or during a big change | Once the direction is clear | Ongoing |
| Engagement | Sessions or a defined project | Monthly retainer | Full-time role |

Many companies hire an agency first and a strategist later, after the campaigns have not worked. In my experience, it is cheaper the other way round.

## When do you need a marketing strategist?

You need one when the business is changing faster than its marketing. The most common moments I see:

- **Entering a new market.** For example, an Australian or European company moving into Saudi Arabia or the UAE. What worked at home rarely translates as it is.
- **Marketing has stopped working.** Activity is steady, but leads are thin, wrong-fit or stall late in the sale.
- **Launching a new offer or moving upmarket.** The old story was built for different buyers.
- **Before hiring an agency.** So the agency has a clear brief instead of guessing.
- **A leadership change, rebrand or fundraising round.** Moments when how you are seen matters most.

## What does this look like in real projects?

Three examples from my own work, anonymised. Each one shows a different side of the job: market entry, positioning and marketing strategy.

![A red-hulled ship sailing toward a desert coastline where the dunes meet the sea](/assets/insights/strategist-market-entry.webp)

### Market entry strategy: is translating your content enough to enter Saudi Arabia?

No. An Australian company planned to launch a training programme for workers in the heavy machinery industry in Saudi Arabia. Their entry plan focused on one question: which languages to translate into.

Our research before launch changed the plan. The workforce spoke Urdu, Arabic and English in Saudi Arabia, and Hindi, Urdu, Arabic and English in Dubai. But the bigger finding was that people expected to learn differently, so the training format had to be redesigned for the market, not just translated.

**The lesson:** a market entry strategy starts with how your audience behaves, not with what your product says. Localisation is more than language.

### Positioning: why does a brand that works at home fail in the GCC?

Because positioning built for one market does not travel automatically. An Italian super-luxury bag brand led its digital marketing with beautiful product imagery. It worked in Europe, but it did not get the same response across the region.

We repositioned the brand for a specific high-end audience in the GCC. Instead of product alone, we built a lifestyle and presence strategy around how that audience actually lives across the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Bahrain and Qatar, while keeping the brand's DNA intact.

**The lesson:** good repositioning changes how a brand shows up in a market without changing who it is.

### Marketing strategy: how do you build an image for a whole market?

With one clear story, repeated consistently. For a bilateral business council, the goal was to make investors see Saudi Arabia as an exceptional place to do business and invest.

Rather than producing more posts, we set a content marketing strategy around a single narrative: why Saudi Arabia, why now and why for this audience. Every piece of content then supported that story.

**The lesson:** a marketing strategy decides the story first. Content only works when it has one message to carry.

The common thread across all three: the first question the client asked was not the one that needed answering. Finding the right question is most of a strategist's job.

## What does working with a marketing strategist look like?

It usually starts with a short conversation, then a focused diagnostic, then a plan your team can run.

The way I work:

1. **A 20-minute clarity conversation** to understand the situation and see if there is a fit.
2. **Diagnostic sessions** to look at how your company is understood today and where the story breaks.
3. **A clear set of priorities:** the narrative, what to fix first and how to measure it.
4. **Execution support if needed,** through your team or a team I manage.

You should leave each session with a decision you can act on, not just a list of ideas.

## How do you find the best marketing strategist in Dubai?

Look for someone who asks more than they tell in the first conversation. Then check these:

- **Regional experience.** The UAE, Saudi Arabia and the wider GCC each have their own buyers, pace and expectations.
- **B2B understanding.** Long sales cycles and buying committees need a different approach from consumer marketing.
- **Both strategy and operations.** A plan your team cannot run is only a document.
- **A clear process and pricing.** You should know what you get from the first session.
- **Honesty about fit.** A good strategist will tell you when you need an agency, not them.

The best marketing strategist in Dubai is not the same for every company. Match the strategist to the problem. If you are entering Saudi Arabia or the wider GCC, repositioning a brand for the region, or fixing marketing that has stopped working, look for someone who has done exactly that here.
